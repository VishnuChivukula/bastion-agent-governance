# EU AI Act, Article 6: when a system is high-risk [VERIFY]

Paraphrased summary; not the legal text. Tier in Bastion: `high`.

Two routes lead to the high-risk class [VERIFY]:
1. The AI system is a safety component of a product (or is itself a product) covered by the EU product-safety legislation listed in Annex I, and that product needs a third-party conformity assessment.
2. The AI system is used for one of the purposes listed in Annex III.

A system listed in Annex III may fall outside the high-risk class if it does not pose a significant risk to health, safety or fundamental rights, for example because it only performs a narrow procedural task, improves the result of a completed human activity, detects decision patterns without replacing the human assessment, or does preparatory work [VERIFY]. That exception does not apply when the system profiles natural persons [VERIFY]. A provider relying on the exception is expected to document the reasoning [VERIFY].

Assessment hints: ask what decision about a person the system makes or materially influences. Human review of every output lowers the operational risk but does not by itself take an Annex III use out of the high-risk class. Autonomy level, data sensitivity and who is affected all matter to the rationale.
