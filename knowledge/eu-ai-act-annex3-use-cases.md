# EU AI Act, Annex III: high-risk use cases [VERIFY]

Paraphrased summary of the areas; not the legal text. Tier in Bastion: `high` (subject to Article 6).

Areas listed, in outline [VERIFY]:
- **Biometrics:** remote biometric identification, biometric categorisation by sensitive attributes, emotion recognition (where not prohibited).
- **Critical infrastructure:** safety components in the management of digital infrastructure, road traffic, water, gas, heating, electricity.
- **Education and vocational training:** deciding admission, evaluating learning outcomes, assessing the appropriate level of education, monitoring and detecting prohibited behaviour of students during tests.
- **Employment and worker management:** recruitment and selection (placing targeted job adverts, analysing and filtering applications, evaluating candidates), decisions on promotion or termination, task allocation based on behaviour or traits, monitoring and evaluating performance.
- **Access to essential private and public services:** evaluating creditworthiness or establishing a credit score of natural persons (with an exception for detecting financial fraud); risk assessment and pricing for life and health insurance; eligibility for public assistance benefits; triage of emergency calls.
- **Law enforcement**, **migration, asylum and border control**, and **administration of justice and democratic processes:** a range of uses such as risk assessments of individuals and evaluating evidence.

Assessment hints: credit-limit decisions about individual customers relate to creditworthiness. Tools that screen CVs relate to recruitment. Proctoring that analyses student behaviour during exams relates to education. Anti-money-laundering and fraud alert handling is close to the fraud-detection exception and to law-enforcement-adjacent uses, so the tier depends on who decides and on what basis: state the uncertainty.
