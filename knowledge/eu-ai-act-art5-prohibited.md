# EU AI Act, Article 5: prohibited practices [VERIFY]

Paraphrased summary; not the legal text. Tier in Bastion: `prohibited`.

Some uses of AI are banned outright rather than regulated. In outline [VERIFY]:
- Techniques that manipulate people below their awareness, or deceive them, in ways that distort behaviour and cause significant harm.
- Exploiting vulnerabilities linked to age, disability or social or economic situation in ways that cause significant harm.
- Social scoring: evaluating or classifying people over time from their social behaviour or personal traits, where the score leads to unfavourable treatment in unrelated contexts or treatment that is disproportionate.
- Predicting that an individual will commit a crime based solely on profiling or personality traits.
- Building facial recognition databases by untargeted scraping of images.
- Inferring emotions of people in the workplace or in education, except for medical or safety reasons.
- Biometric categorisation that infers sensitive characteristics such as race, political opinions or sexual orientation.
- Real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law enforcement, outside narrow exceptions.

Assessment hints: check the purpose, not the technology. A system that measures employee emotion to manage productivity is the workplace emotion case. A public-authority scheme that ranks citizens' general trustworthiness is the social scoring case.
